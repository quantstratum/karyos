### Added

- Drops on the `etf` route of timed sends (karyos#625, ADR-0109 points 6 to 8): a publication on the route keeps, per lane, a ledger of the datagrams it handed to the etf queue (`(txtime, position range)`, bounded by the lane's schedule capacity; a full ledger holds the lane's next timed head). Each sender duty drains every lane's error queue first, and a drop report drops every entry of its time: a reliable lane's ranges are caught up late on the immediate route in position order by the repair path's catch-up, one job at a time per lane; an unreliable lane's are a counted gap at the receiver. Then an entry with no report by `txtime + advance + 100 ms` is retired as sent. A report that arrives after its entry was retired resubmits nothing: it raises the publication's `etf_late_drop_reports` (`EmbeddedDriver::etf_late_drop_reports`) and records one `EtfLateDropReport` (code 12) in the driver's error log, keyed by the aggregate registration and the report's time. `scripts/privileged-test.sh` gains a veth pair into its peer namespace behind a `prio` qdisc with an `etf` leaf, and runs the driver's end to end test there: over a real leaf, a reliable publication whose datagram is dropped is delivered complete, and an unreliable one shows the gap.
