### Added

- Read resources for clocks, calibrations and rate limits (R-CORE-40, karyos#741), new in agent API v1 with every existing answer unchanged:
  - **`clocks DIR`:** the clocks of the driver's local trace journals the agent follows, each with its provenance, domain, calibration epoch, last time and largest uncertainty, and the calibrations the agent can see with their validity ranges. A clock without a valid calibration reads as unavailable with its reason (`no_calibration`, `not_observed`, `expired`, `not_yet_valid`, `ambiguous`), never as a number, and no latency is derived. Until karyos#118 produces calibrations, every clock reads `no_calibration` and the list is empty with `"calibrations_unavailable":"no_producer"`: a clocks view legitimately shows no cross-host calibration.
  - **`rate-limits DIR`:** per network publication, the rate policy version in force and its two limiters, the publication's bucket and the control reserve's, each `limited` with its policy and refusal count, `none` (no such bucket) or `not_counted` (a driver without the cells), so a count of 0 is only a bucket that refused nothing.
  - **Counter cells:** each network publication gains twelve cells after `network rate policies refused` (`network rate limiters`, `network rate policy version`, then per bucket its message and byte rates and bursts and `... refusals`); the ten policy cells are gauges and the two refusal cells counts; size the counters region for 18 cells per network publication.
  - **Capabilities:** `clocks` and `rate-limits`, served to every role. Both reads use what the agent already holds, never a mapping or the data path.
