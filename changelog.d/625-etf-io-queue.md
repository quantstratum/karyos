### Added

- The `etf` route's I/O contract in `karyos-io` (karyos#625, ADR-0109 points 1, 2, 3, 5 and the drain half of 7): `TxOptions::txtime`, a per-datagram send time in `CLOCK_TAI` nanoseconds that the ledger accepts only on a queue opened as an etf queue (`UnsupportedOption` elsewhere); `TxQueueConfig::etf`, an `EtfQueue` declaration (interface index, `SO_PRIORITY`, qdisc `delta`), which the `io_uring` and AF_XDP backends refuse with the new `QueueError::EtfUnavailable`; and, in the sockets backend, an etf queue of an endpoint opened with `SocketsConfig::etf_route` owns an `EtfSocket` that joins the endpoint socket's `SO_REUSEPORT` group after the classic BPF program `ret #0` is attached to it (`SO_ATTACH_REUSEPORT_CBPF`, a new safe call in `linux_sys`), so its datagrams leave from the endpoint's own address and port and no inbound datagram reaches it. A refused `SO_TXTIME`, `SO_PRIORITY` or attach refuses the queue; an etf queue on another endpoint or to a multicast destination is refused with `QueueError::EtfRefused`, and `etf_route` with a multicast group is an invalid configuration. A flush of an etf queue sends its datagrams in submission order as runs by route: a time still ahead on the etf socket, a time already past (by one `CLOCK_TAI` read per flush) or no time on the endpoint socket; a partial run ends the flush as a partial send does, and a past time is reported with the new `TxEntry::late`. `SocketsTx::drain_etf` drains an etf queue's error queue into decoded drops. `EtfSocket::send` now takes the destination as a `SocketAddrAny` and returns the bytes sent.
