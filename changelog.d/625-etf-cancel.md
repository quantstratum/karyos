### Changed

- Cancel-on-deadline on a timed network publication (karyos#625, ADR-0109 point 8): it is no longer refused (`TimedNetworkUnsupported::CancelOnDeadline` is removed). The sender cannot seal a buffer, so a lane that misses its deadline, a head whose time plus its tolerance passes before it is exposed or, on the `etf` route, a datagram the qdisc dropped, is aborted at the sender (nothing more of it is sent, counted in `deadline aborts`) and reported once with the new `AgentEvent::DeadlineMissed`; the conductor's schedule violation hook seals it with `SealReason::DeadlineMissed` if it still names the live incarnation (a lane already sealed keeps its first reason), with no fault and no client retired. A submitted message keeps its `Submitted` record (G7). On the `etf` route a drop under cancel-on-deadline is neither caught up nor a gap. A drop whose report the kernel lost is not seen, so it is repaired or a gap, not an abort. The privileged end to end test covers the case over a real `etf` leaf.
