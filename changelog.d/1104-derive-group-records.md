### Changed

- Every recording group journal record is derived with `karyos-codec-derive` instead of written by hand (karyos#1104); their wire encoding is unchanged and every existing vector decodes and encodes exactly. The derive gains what they need: a versioned message (`#[message(versioned)]`, a `decode` taking `v2: bool`) whose `#[field(offset = N, since = 2)]` fields read as their default from a version 1 block; reserved bytes (`#[message(zero = [(OFFSET, WIDTH), ...])]`), refused unless zero; variable tied fields (`#[field(with = PATH, spans = [...], var)]`), read with the decoder in the variable fields' order; and tied fields written from the whole message, so two fields that share a byte are written together. `MessageEvent` now declares `payload` before `extension`, the order of their bytes on the wire.
