### Added

- The service's atomic snapshot callback (R-CORE-9, karyos#746): `ClusteredService::snapshot` writes the service's state as one or more versioned parts into a `ServiceSnapshot` (bytes and part slots allocated once; `ServiceSnapshot::reserved_bytes` is the budget with 48 bytes of metadata per part), and `ClusteredService::restore` takes them back, all or nothing; both default to a service with no state. `ServiceContainer::snapshot` composes, between two applies, the sessions, timers and guards sections with the service's parts, all from the last applied index (`ComposedSnapshot::index`), and `ServiceContainer::restore` checks every container section, hands the service its parts, and only then replaces its tables, so a refused restore changes nothing (`RestoreError`, `RestoreRefused` for an unknown part, a newer version, a missing or malformed part). A section of a kind the store does not know is now `SnapshotError::UnknownSection`, told apart from damage. A sim test over C2's crashing disk shows a composed snapshot of three service parts leaves, at every crash point and failed flush, the previous snapshot or the complete new one, restoring to the same state as replaying the log. CRC-32C checks detect corruption and claim no tamper evidence.
