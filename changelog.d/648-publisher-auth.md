### Added

- Publisher side of the wire authentication profile and its establishment, unicast (part of karyos#648; quantstratum-docs: karyos/adr/0086-wire-authentication-profile-v1.md sections 2, 4, 6, 7 and 7a). An authenticated publication holds its channel's SETUP proof (domain 5) and, per receiver it may serve, the pairwise key's RSP_SETUP proof (domain 6), a domain 4 verifier and the boot id table of the publisher rule (`wire_auth::BootTable`), allocated at admission. Its receivers' envelopes reach the sender agent undecoded (`ControlItem::Sealed`, a fixed array) and are verified before anything reads them: an RSP_SETUP under its pair's proof and then the publisher rule (a retired boot id, a previous file epoch, a full table, or a bound boot id under another nonce bind nothing), an SM, NAK or RTTM REQUEST under the bound receiver's domain 4 key, checked and only committed once its inner control is that receiver's. A new binding installs the session sealer (`Party::Sender` with the receiver's boot id and nonce) through `Outbound::install_sealer`. Its SETUP ANNOUNCE and ANSWER go sealed in domain 5, its PATH_HB, ERR and RTTM REPLY in domain 1, never bare. On the receiver, an authenticated subscription verifies SETUP ANNOUNCE and ANSWER under the SETUP proof before any challenge is created or consumed (a verified one may also bind a later path), seals its CHALLENGEs and SOLICITs in domain 6, and derives each image's keys at install (an image whose keys cannot be made is not installed). Bare controls for an authenticated publication or subscription are dropped and counted; refusals are counted by reason. Trusted paths are byte-identical. Nothing makes a channel authenticated yet, and `authenticated` channels are still refused.
