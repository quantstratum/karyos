### Added

- Retention policy, legal hold and the crash-safe purge of the Archive (SPEC.md §8.4, "Retention policy" and "Purge and legal hold"; R-CORE-22; karyos#667): `karyos_archive::integrity::retention` adds versioned retention policies chained in the integrity journal and bound to a recording or a channel and stream class, with no built-in regulatory period; holds that apply without waiting on the operations journal, are reported applied only with evidence, survive restart while pending, and whose release fails closed; purges gated on authorisation, holds first, expiry and `min_copies` of verified copies elsewhere, every refusal journalled with its reason; and a purge over `SegmentStore::purge_before` that journals its intent first, records the purged range's seal digest in the integrity journal, and is rechecked on recovery so a hold or policy change after a crash stops the deletion. `--archive-min-free` is unchanged.
