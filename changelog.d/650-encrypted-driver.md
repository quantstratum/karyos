### Added

- Driver integration of the `encrypted` protection class (karyos#650, karyos#651, ADR-0114): key files of `profile_id=16`, one class per file; SETUP ANSWER variant 3 (`key_epoch`, `party_epoch`), required on every encrypted answer; the encrypted publication and subscription paths, sealing every DATA and control and never sending one bare, with RSP_SETUP bound by its pairwise proof and domains 4 and 6 carrying the pairwise trailer; the receive order of section 8 (outer class, sequence cap, open rate, key age, open, inner class and frame checks, mark, then effect); epoch rolls with a 10 s overlap, and the resynchronisation of a bound receiver (section 6b) through a challenge-bound SOLICIT sealed in domain 6. Each rejection reason has its own counter, an envelope on a stream that is not encrypted is counted as unexpected, and a stripped, mixed-class or plaintext-SETUP datagram is dropped under its own reason. No channel is encrypted yet: the class still fails at channel parse, and an `encrypted` channel's `mtu` is at most 1408.
