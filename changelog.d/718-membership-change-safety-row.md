### Added

- `test_cluster_membership_change_safety` (`crates/karyos-sim/tests/membership_change_safety.rs`, karyos#718) is the named test of SPEC §13's "Membership change safety" row, on the custom Raft core. It covers adds and removes under partitions and leader crashes (M2, M3, M4, M6), a lagging learner refused with `ChangeRefused::Lagging` (M5), and a standby promoted over the Cluster log replication while the partitioned old primary keeps its quorum and a client (S3). It asserts the row's pass conditions in order: verified fencing first, every entry up to the verified position kept, the unknown interval and the requests in flight reported, the old generation refused. Each condition has a mutant it catches. The test needs no authenticated profile. The harness now records `Observation::Split` when a case splits the network, so a check can tell what came after the partition.
