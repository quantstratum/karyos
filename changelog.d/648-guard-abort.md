### Added

- Signed GUARD and SESSION_ABORT, and SESSION_ABORT_ACK in domain 4, on unicast (part of karyos#648; quantstratum-docs: karyos/adr/0086-wire-authentication-profile-v1.md sections 2, 6, 7 and 8, karyos/design/authenticated-control-schemas.md sections 3 to 6, ADR-0107). `signed_control::SignedIntake` runs the receive order of the signed domains: the verification budget (one per duty cycle, 256 per second per driver, 4 per second per source over a preallocated table; excess dropped and counted before any verification), a lookup in a bounded, preallocated cache of the SHA-256 digests of applied datagrams (a hit resends the idempotent ACK and changes nothing), `verify_strict` under the authority key the envelope names, and the authority checks of its key file (`authority_id`, the guard in `guards`, `abort` for SESSION_ABORT); the digest is inserted only after the state change. A verified SESSION_ABORT closes the bound authenticated image of its incarnation once and sends a SESSION_ABORT_ACK sealed under the image's domain 4 key, which the publisher verifies under the bound receiver's key; `SenderRole::request_abort` signs and sends one to each bound receiver. `remote_guard::RemoteGuard` follows another driver's guard: it starts closed, recovers only through a signed STATUS answering its own unexpired nonce for its current boot id (floor `max(durable mark, proven epoch)`, a STATUS below the mark latches it closed), raises and persists its durable mark (`remote_guard::GuardMarks`, an atomic replace in the state directory) before acting, opens only on a confirming STATUS and lapses one lifetime after the last one. Bare GUARD, SESSION_ABORT and SESSION_ABORT_ACK stay gated. Nothing provisions an authority or a followed guard yet; `require-abort-ack` and `authenticated` channels are still refused.

- The guard authority's side and remote guards on the sender gates (part of karyos#648; quantstratum-docs: karyos/design/authenticated-control-schemas.md sections 4.2 to 4.4, ADR-0107, ADR-0086 section 7). `guard_authority::GuardAuthority` answers a bare STATUS_CHALLENGE with a GUARD STATUS signed in domain 2 over the authority id, the guard, the challenger's boot id and receiver id, its nonce and the guard's current word, sent only to the receiver's configured endpoint and only for a challenge from that address; before any signature the challenge passes the per-driver budget (256 per second, 4 per duty cycle) and the per-destination budget (4 per second, burst 4, one preallocated bucket per configured endpoint shared by its guards), and a refusal is counted by reason. A configuration beyond the authority's or an endpoint's capacity is refused at load. A followed guard mirrors its state into a guard word that a publication's `GuardGate` reads: open only while the guard is open and confirmed, so a signed close or a lapse stops every submission as a local close does.
