### Added

- Receive side of the wire authentication profile, unicast (part of karyos#648; quantstratum-docs: karyos/adr/0086-wire-authentication-profile-v1.md sections 2, 4, 6 and 7a). An authenticated image holds its domain 1 `Verifier`, salted with the receiver's boot id and the establishment's challenge nonce (now carried from the answer to the install), and its domain 4 `Sealer`. Every unicast datagram that is an envelope is opened by its binding's key (fields, tag, then replay window) before any inner frame is classified; a refused one, an envelope for no authenticated binding, a bare DATA, heartbeat, PATH_HB, ERR or RTTM REPLY for an authenticated binding, and an opened envelope whose inner frames are not its own binding's are dropped and counted (`auth_rejected`, `auth_unbound`, `auth_bare`, `auth_inner_invalid`) with no other state change. Its SMs, NAKs and RTTM REQUESTs carry the image's sealer to the sender agent and leave only sealed in domain 4; one that cannot be sealed is never sent bare (`seal_failures`), and it sends no group NAK copy. Trusted images and controls are unchanged. Nothing makes an image authenticated yet, and `authenticated` channels are still refused.
