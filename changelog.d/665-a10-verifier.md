### Added

- `karyos-catalog verify` and the Archive's integrity verifier (SPEC.md §8.4, "Verification"; karyos#665): `karyos_archive::integrity::verify` recomputes each sealed range's digest, walks the chain, checks the key registry and signed heads under a trust root supplied outside the export, compares the chain with its anchors, and reports every range as `verified`, `beyond_anchor`, `unsealed`, `purged` or `inconsistent` with explicit reasons, the first inconsistent record and the signature and anchor coverage. Missing trust is `inconsistent` with reason `untrusted_evidence`, apart from proved corruption; an index shortened below a seal is `inconsistent`; only `verified` carries a tamper-evidence statement; `purged` states that it proves nothing about payload bytes; every other outcome is C-EXTRACT's "unverifiable"; time evidence is reported in a separate field. A configurable scrub reverifies on a schedule and raises an event per mismatch.
