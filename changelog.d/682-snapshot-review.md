### Fixed

- Snapshot transfers and compaction (the karyos#682 review of #1261): a streamed snapshot is taken only by the store that began it (`SnapshotError::Foreign`); `SnapshotStore::seal` makes it durable unpublished so a receiver reads it back and checks it before `publish_sealed`, which keeps a pinned snapshot from the purge; a failed begin or seal cleans up and fails no store. The harness transfer carries a transfer id and frozen metadata, ignores stale pieces, acknowledges duplicates, drops gaps, keeps at most `SNAPSHOT_WINDOW` pieces unacknowledged, verifies the snapshot's end before its last piece and its digest and base before publishing, expires stalled or overtaken transfers, and keeps a moving transfer however often its core asks. A missing frame or a failed purge is reported; the reference core sends a snapshot to a follower whose next entry is the first one held.
