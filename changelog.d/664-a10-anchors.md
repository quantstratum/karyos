### Added

- External anchors of the Archive's integrity chain and the copy catalog's immutability record (SPEC.md §8.4, "External anchors" and "Immutability enforcement"; karyos#664): `karyos_archive::integrity::anchor` adds versioned anchors of the replica journal, Cluster log, operator export and object-lock kinds with an off-host counter and receipt, refuses a local replica, a circular pair and the deferred RFC 3161 kind as external, detects truncation after the last anchored head and a chain that breaks below an anchor, rollback below an anchor, equivocation and store counter rollback per store kind and location, accepts a revoked key's head only with evidence that it was anchored before the revocation, reports an old anchor as covering its prefix only and a missing anchor as a stated absence, checks ancestry and an anchor policy, and publishes anchors on a schedule; `karyos_archive::integrity::copies` records each copy's state and immutability enforcement, chained as copy changes and replayed in journal order on reopen, and states when the storage cannot enforce it.
