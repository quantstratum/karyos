### Added

- The Archive's control protocol gains schema 1 version 2, the read-only retention state query of ADR-0101 (karyos#967): `RetentionState` (template 10) names a stream of the requester's own, and the Archive answers there with a `RetentionDescriptor` (105) per recording (its hold: none, held, pending or unknown; its recorded close time; the earliest policy expiry from it; the policy) and a `RetentionSummary` (106) with the `--archive-min-free` floor and the free bytes. The shared response stream carries at most a version 1 refusal, so no version 1 reader decodes a version 2 page; a version 1 Archive answers code 5. `karyosd --archive-control <req>,<resp>[,<state>]` configures the stream, and the management agent (`--archive DIR=REQ,RESP[,STATE]`) reads it after each catalog listing and shows it in its view and JSON as `catalog.retention`.
