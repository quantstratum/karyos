### Added

- A timed network publication on the `etf` route of timed sends (karyos#625, ADR-0109 points 1, 2 and 5): `ScheduleOptions::route` takes `TimedRoute::Etf { advance }`, the declared tolerated advance, which travels to publishers in word 7 of the schedule ring's binding (0 is the software route); the channel declares its `etf` leaf with the new URI keys `etf-priority` and `etf-delta` (`UdpConfig::etf`, an `EtfDeclaration`), on a unicast `endpoint` with an `interface` address. The driver refuses, typed (`TimedNetworkUnsupported`), the route without a declaration (`EtfUndeclared`), a declaration without the route (`EtfWithoutRoute`), a `delta` above the advance (`EtfDeltaAboveAdvance`), a priority another etf publication declares on the same interface (`EtfPriorityShared`), and the route off Linux or off a unicast destination (`EtfUnavailable`); a zero advance is `InvalidSchedule`. Such a publication sends from an endpoint of its own opened with `etf_route`, its lane queues are etf queues, a timed head is eligible its advance before its time, and it is committed with its time converted to `CLOCK_TAI` by an offset read once a sender cycle (`SocketsTx::tai_now_ns`); while `CLOCK_TAI` cannot be read the lane stages nothing. Drops, cancel-on-deadline and the end to end tests follow in the next parts of karyos#625.
