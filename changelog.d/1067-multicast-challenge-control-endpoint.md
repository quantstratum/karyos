### Changed

- A multicast subscription's CHALLENGE, and every resend of it, now leaves from the driver's unicast control endpoint, which its SOLICITs already use (ADR-0096; karyos#1067). This is visible on the wire: the UDP source port of a multicast CHALLENGE is now the control endpoint's port instead of the group port, and the bytes are unchanged. Before, the drivers on one host shared the group port, so the publisher charged all their CHALLENGEs to one `setup_answer_rate` bucket. Two drivers joining 20 streams each lost 13 to 14 CHALLENGEs, and one of them bound half its streams late, through SOLICIT. A key without a control endpoint still challenges from its arrival socket, counted as `challenges_from_arrival`. A firewall that admits RSP_SETUP only from the group port must also admit the control endpoint's port.
