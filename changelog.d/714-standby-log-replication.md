### Added

- The Cluster log is a replication source (ADR-0120 decisions 3 and 4, karyos#714): `RaftLog::replication_start` (the current seal epoch's first seal boundary at or after the store's start, a frame start), `RaftLog::replication_seals` (the epoch and its `ClusterLog` seals in order), `RaftLog::read_stored`, and `RaftLog::replication_end`, which names why a request must end (`ReplicationEnd::NotLeading`, `NewEpoch`, `PurgedPast`). `log::read_frame` exposes the log's frame check (magic, length, CRC-32C) on bytes.
- `karyos_cluster::standby`, the standby cluster's follower of the primary's Cluster log (ADR-0120 decisions 4 to 9, karyos#714): one copy per source node and epoch, one active source at a time; each seal verified on the copy's own bytes through the `SealCheck` seam (`DigestCheck` checks the content digest), then its frames, their chain and the anchor at the verified position `V`, refused whole with `V` unchanged; a gap stops the follower; `V` rebuilt from the standby's own log (`verified_in`, `Standby::rebuild`); a recovery point that is never zero (`Behind`, `AtSealedEnd`, `Unknown`); and fenced promotion (an operator-confirmed stop of every old member or an expired lease) that raises the generation and reports `V`, the copy's ends, the gap as a lower bound and the in-flight requests as unknown outcomes. Automatic promotion and a synchronous standby profile stay later decisions.
- The simulator's standby scenarios S1 to S4, S6 and S7 run over the Cluster log replication, with the checksummed feed kept as their control run (karyos#714): S6's damage is applied to the bytes in transit, S7's restarted standby leader rebuilds `V` from its own log, and a primary leader change starts a new copy.
