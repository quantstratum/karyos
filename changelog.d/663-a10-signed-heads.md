### Added

- The trust bootstrap, the key registry and signed chain heads of the Archive (SPEC.md §8.4, "Trust bootstrap", "Signed heads and custody" and "Key registry"; karyos#663): `karyos_archive::integrity::trust` adds canonical versioned Ed25519 head bytes under an archival domain tag, a key registry whose entries the external authority endorses and whose digests are chained in the integrity journal, registration before first use, a registry rebuilt on reopen only from the entries the journal chains, with the registration timing checked on replay as on append nothing read or cut through a poisoned journal, an append accepted only from a registry whose whole history is the one the journal chains, and ledger writes refused through a stale handle, retirement, evidence-backed revocation with pre-revocation anchoring, and honest custody labels; `karyos_archive::integrity::signing` adds a coalesce-or-refuse signing backlog that never blocks sealing and counts a head as signed only once it belongs to the journal's chain, verifies under the registry and is stored durably, withdraws coverage a change of the registry's contents invalidates and, after a restart, only if the stored head verifies against the registry and the chain, keeping records left uncovered overdue, the coverage lag and an `anchor_interval` alarm on the oldest uncovered record, with an unconfigured interval refused.
