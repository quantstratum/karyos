### Added

- A durable calibration journal (karyos#1137, ADR-0098 items 3 and 5, ADR-0110): `karyos-tools calibrate append --journal <dir>` appends records (the 18 words `calibrate phc` prints, given as words or piped on standard input, one whole record per `record` line) and `karyosd --archive-calibration-journal <dir>` gives the Archive agent that journal as its calibration source, read again at each snapshot, so records committed while the daemon runs reach groups' time evidence without the host calling `ArchiveHandle::set_calibration_source`. The journal is a directory holding one Archive segment store recording (recording 1, one 144-byte record per write, each covered by its index entry's CRC-32C, in segments a whole number of records long so no record straddles two) and a `durable` file, the committed position and the journal's generation with their CRC-32C, replaced atomically after the store's sync. Readers read `durable` first and take only the records up to it: a record past it is invisible and cut by the next writer, and a store that holds fewer records than it commits is a loss, never a shorter lossless journal. The generation is fixed at creation; rotating means a new directory. Under `writer.lock` the writer refuses a record whose `valid_from` is not after the last of its type and clock (ADR-0098's completeness boundary), skips one already present so a retried batch never duplicates, and appends nothing of a batch with any refusal, each reported per record. New directories have their parents synced. A record type this build does not know is kept and skipped by readers. `karyos_archive::integrity::calibration_journal` provides `CalibrationJournal`, `read`, `read_durable` and `JournalSource`.
