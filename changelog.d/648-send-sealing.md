### Added

- Sealing on the send path of the wire authentication profile, unicast (part of karyos#648; quantstratum-docs: karyos/adr/0086-wire-authentication-profile-v1.md sections 2, 5, 6 and 7a). An authenticated publication (`Outbound::require_auth`, then `Outbound::install_sealer` with the session's `wire_auth::Sealer`) seals every DATA batch, PAD and ABORTED record, heartbeat, EOS and repair under a fresh envelope sequence of one key; its frames keep the inner budget, the MTU less the 48-byte envelope, and the datagram stays within the MTU. Until its key is installed, or once the key can seal no more, it reads and sends nothing (`seal_waits`). A repair is sealed per route copy, never on the shared read. Multi-destination, dynamic and routed publications refuse authentication. `ControlRoutes::send_sealed` sends a control sealed by a `ControlSeal` (a session `Sealer` for domains 1 and 4, a `Proof` for SETUP and RSP_SETUP). Nothing installs a sealer yet, and `authenticated` channels are still refused.
