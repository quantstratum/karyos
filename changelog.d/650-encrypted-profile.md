### Added

- The protocol layer of the `encrypted` protection class, AES-256-GCM-SIV (karyos#650, ADR-0114): `karyos_protocol::protection::aes_gcm_siv` seals and opens the encrypted envelope (frame type `0x21`, profile 16, 84 bytes with the tag) with keys derived per domain, party and epoch, a counter nonce that never repeats or passes its cap, a replay window checked before and marked after inner validation, the 1408-byte mtu cap and the pairwise trailer of domains 4 and 6. A rejected datagram changes no state, and every downgrade case of karyos#651 has its own reason (`EncRejection`). No channel enables the class yet: the channel gate keeps `encrypted` refused until the driver slice. New dependency `aes-gcm-siv` 0.12.1, with `aes` 0.9.4 and `polyval` 0.7.3 declared directly so their key schedules are erased (owner decision D53).
- `karyos_protocol::protection::hkdf`, the workspace's one HKDF-SHA-256, over the erasing `HmacKey`: moved down from `karyos-driver`'s `wire_keys::Context::derive`, which now calls it, and used by the encrypted profile. The `hkdf` crate only cross-checks it in tests, since it leaves its intermediates unerased.
