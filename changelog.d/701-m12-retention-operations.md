### Added

- The Archive's retention operations through the agent (M12; part of karyos#701): `karyos-agent` serves `legal-hold RECORDING [REASON]`, `hold-release RECORDING [REASON]`, `purge RECORDING BEFORE [REASON]` and `seal-verify RECORDING`, each journalled before it is sent and after it is answered, with its operator and parameters digest, under new journal kinds 9 to 16 (the existing codes are unchanged). A hold is sent even when the journal is unavailable; a hold release and a purge refuse without a durable request record. A hold needs an operator, a risk officer or an administrator; a release and a purge need an administrator; any role may verify. Refusals and integrity outcomes are the Archive's, reported verbatim, through the new `karyos_agent::retention::RetentionBackend` trait.
